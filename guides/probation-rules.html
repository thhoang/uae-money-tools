Probation period rules in the UAE

A probation period lets an employer assess a new hire and lets the employee test the job. It also changes some of the rules that apply when employment ends, most importantly for people who think about quitting early. This guide covers what the UAE Government Portal and Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 say, and clearly marks the points where official pages differ, so you know what to double-check with MOHRE.

How long can probation be?

The Labour Law says probation must not exceed six months and cannot be extended for a further term. If your offer letter or contract says anything longer, or an employer tells you in month six that probation will continue, raise it with MOHRE. The probation period should be written into your contract; if it is not, ask for it to be added before you sign.

Annual leave during probation

The Government Portal's summary says the employer may grant you leave from your annual leave balance during the probation period, and that you have the right to be compensated for the remaining annual leave balance if you did not pass probation. Separately, the portal says that for service of more than six months and under one year, you earn 2 days of leave for each month of service. The pages we used do not state an accrual for the first six months, so ask your employer what applies in your contract.

Leaving during probation

This is the part that catches people. Under Ministerial Resolution No. 47 of 2022, a worker who quits during the probation period, where the employer has not breached its obligations, cannot obtain a new work permit for one year from departure. The same ban applies if a proven "work abandonment" report exists against the worker. In plain terms: resigning in the first six months to join a competitor can block your next UAE work permit for a year.

The Government Portal also notes the ban is aimed at quitting without cause. If your employer is the one in breach (for example by not paying wages), the position is different and you should take advice before acting. The law also provides a route for leaving without notice where the employer breaches its obligations, but it has strict conditions, including notifying MOHRE first; see resignation vs termination.

What notice applies during probation?

Here official pages are not fully consistent. The main termination rule (Article 43) requires written notice of 30 to 90 days. For probation, some official pages describe a minimum of one month for a resignation to join another UAE employer and 14 days if you are leaving the country, while other readings of the Decree-Law give the employer 14 days' written notice to end probation. We have not been able to confirm a single authoritative wording, so this site does not give a number. Check the current text on the MOHRE website or call MOHRE before you rely on one.

What about gratuity and probation?

Gratuity needs at least one year of continuous service, and probation is at most six months, so someone who leaves during probation does not qualify. Whether the probation period counts towards the one-year service for someone who stays is not addressed in the pages we reviewed; ask your employer in writing, because it affects the date you first become eligible. Our gratuity calculator counts service from your first day of work.

Practical checklist for new joiners

  1. Find the probation length in your contract. If it is over six months, query it.
  2. Write down your first day of work; it drives leave and gratuity dates.
  3. Ask whether any annual leave can be taken during probation, and how a balance would be paid if probation is not passed.
  4. Before resigning in the first six months, check the one-year work-permit ban. If you have an offer from another employer, ask it whether the ban affects your permit.
  5. Keep every written message about performance. If you are ended on grounds of misconduct, the employer should follow the written-investigation procedure in Article 44.
  6. Keep your payslips and the signed contract.

For employers and HR

Record probation start and end dates, deliver notices in writing, and keep the evidence of any breach alleged against the employee. If you end employment during probation, pay outstanding wages and any unused leave compensation due, and give the worker a clear final statement.

Example

Omar joins on 1 March with a six-month probation. In June he receives a better offer. He resigns during probation. Because his employer has not breached the contract, he risks the one-year work-permit ban counted from the date he leaves. If instead he waits until 1 September, probation is over and the usual notice rules for resignation apply (30 to 90 days, paid at full wage).

Frequently asked questions

How long can a probation period be in the UAE?

No more than six months, and it cannot be extended.

Can I take annual leave during probation?

The employer may grant leave from your annual leave balance during probation. If you do not pass probation you have the right to compensation for the remaining annual leave balance, according to the Government Portal summary.

Can I be banned from working if I quit during probation?

Yes. A worker who quits during probation without the employer having breached its contract cannot get a new work permit for one year from departure (Ministerial Resolution No. 47 of 2022).

Do I get gratuity if I leave during probation?

No. Gratuity requires at least one year of continuous service and probation is at most six months.
