Unpaid leave and gratuity in the UAE

If you took time off without pay, it affects your end-of-service gratuity. The UAE Government Portal says that days of absence without pay are excluded from the service period used to calculate gratuity. This guide shows what that means in numbers and what to check on your own records.

The rule

Gratuity under Article 51 is based on your length of continuous service. The official summary states that days of absence without pay are not counted towards it. In practice: take the days between your first and last working day, subtract your unpaid days, and use the result as your service period. The rest of the formula is unchanged: 21 days of basic pay per year for the first 5 years and 30 days after, capped at two years' wages.

Worked example: 60 unpaid days over five years

Basic salary AED 7,000, first day 1 March 2019, last day 1 March 2024. That span is 1,827 days (5.005 years) with no deductions.

The one-year edge case

No gratuity is due for less than one year of continuous service. So unpaid days can push you under the line. Example: first day 1 January 2024, last day 1 January 2025 (366 days) with 10 unpaid days leaves 356 days, or 0.975 years. That is under one year, so the gratuity is AED 0 even though you were employed for a calendar year. If you are near your first anniversary, check this before you hand in notice.

What counts as "unpaid"?

The official wording is "absence without pay". The pages we rely on do not list every type of leave, so we do not classify individual cases. If you are unsure whether a particular absence counts, check your payslips: if you were paid for the day, it is a paid day. If you were not, ask MOHRE or your employer how it was recorded. Do not assume that all absences are treated alike.

Checklist before you leave

  1. List every period of unpaid leave and count the days (calendar days, including weekends within the period).
  2. Compare your list with the employer's records. If they differ, ask for the attendance report.
  3. Enter the unpaid days in the gratuity calculator and note the difference.
  4. Keep copies of approvals for any leave you believe was paid.

Once your counted service passes five years, each unpaid day is worth more, because later years earn 30 days instead of 21.

Why employers and employees disagree about it

Most disputes are about records, not the rule. An employee remembers a month "off" that the employer logged as paid annual leave, or the reverse. Annual leave is fully paid, so it counts towards service; an absence the employer recorded as unpaid does not. If your employer's service calculation is shorter than yours, ask for the dates of each unpaid period. If they cannot show them, say so in writing before you sign the final settlement. If you are the employer, keep dated approvals for each unpaid period so the figure can be proved.

Steps if the numbers do not match

  1. Calculate your own figure with the calculator and save a screenshot or note of the inputs.
  2. Ask the employer in writing for their service dates, unpaid days and daily wage.
  3. Compare the three inputs one by one: basic salary used, service days counted, unpaid days removed.
  4. If you still disagree, contact MOHRE through its official channels. The official end-of-service page expects payment within 14 days of the end of the contract, so raise the issue promptly.

Does unpaid leave affect annual leave?

The pages we used do not say how unpaid leave interacts with annual leave accrual, so this site does not model it. The annual leave calculator counts calendar service days only. If you took long unpaid leave, check your contract and ask MOHRE.

Frequently asked questions

Do unpaid leave days count towards gratuity?

No. Days of absence without pay are excluded from the service period, according to the UAE Government Portal.

Can unpaid leave make me lose my gratuity entirely?

It can if it brings your counted service under one year, because no gratuity is due for less than one year of continuous service.

How do I enter unpaid days in the calculator?

Use the "Unpaid leave days" field in the gratuity calculator; it subtracts them before applying the formula.

Are weekends in the middle of unpaid leave counted?

We count calendar days. If your employer counts differently, ask for the calculation in writing.
