Resignation vs termination in the UAE: what you get in each case

Whether you resign or your employer ends your contract, the same core steps apply: written notice, a notice period of 30 to 90 days, and a final settlement. The differences are in who owes what if notice is not served, when dismissal without notice is allowed, and what happens to your next work permit. This guide sets out what the UAE Government Portal and the Labour Law text say, and flags what we could not confirm.

The common rules (Article 43)

  • Either party can end the contract "for any legitimate reason" with written notice.
  • The notice period is at least 30 days and no more than 90 days. The contract stays in force until it ends.
  • The worker receives the full wage during the notice period, based on the most recent salary.
  • A party that does not serve the full notice owes the other a notice-period allowance, even if no loss resulted. Estimate it with the notice period pay calculator.
  • If the employer ends the contract, the worker gets one unpaid day per week to look for a job, and must declare the chosen day at least 3 days in advance.

Dismissal without notice (Article 44)

An employer may end a contract without notice only on specific grounds. The Government Portal lists examples including forged documents, deliberate damage to property, and absence of more than 20 non-consecutive days in a year or more than 7 consecutive days. Failing basic duties after two warnings is also a ground. Dismissal requires a written investigation first, and the dismissal notice must be written, justified and handed to the employee. If you are dismissed this way, ask for the written reasons and the investigation record.

Arbitrary dismissal

If a court finds dismissal was arbitrary, it can order compensation, which must not exceed the employee's wage for three months, based on the last wage. You can also claim gratuity, notice-period dues and other unpaid dues.

Leaving without notice (Article 45)

A worker may leave without serving notice and still keep end-of-service entitlements if the employer breaches its obligations. The worker must notify MOHRE 14 working days before quitting, and the employer must have failed to fix the breach after MOHRE notified it. Workers who are assaulted or harassed must report within five working days. Do not rely on this route without taking advice: the conditions are strict.

Work-permit ban

Under Ministerial Resolution No. 47 of 2022, a worker who quits during probation without the employer having breached its contract cannot get a new work permit for one year from departure. The same one-year ban applies where a proven "work abandonment" report exists. See the probation rules guide.

What about gratuity if I resign?

The Government Portal's end-of-service page states the gratuity formula by length of service and, in the text we reviewed, does not list a reduction for resigning. Our older guides say that the old reductions for resigning from an unlimited contract no longer apply; we have flagged this wording for review against the consolidated law text. If your employer applies a reduction, ask which article they rely on.

Worked example

You earn AED 9,000 a month (all basic), have 4 years of service and resign on 30 days' notice, but leave after 10. The daily rate at 30 days a month is AED 300; 20 unserved days give an allowance of about AED 6,000 that the employer may claim from you. Your gratuity is 4 × 21 = 84 days × AED 295.89 = AED 24,854.79, and any unused leave is paid in addition. Use the final settlement calculator to net these off.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the notice period for resignation in the UAE?

Between 30 and 90 days under Article 43; the exact period is in your contract.

Am I paid during my notice period?

Yes, the full wage, based on your latest salary.

Can my employer fire me without notice?

Only on the grounds listed in Article 44, after a written investigation and with a written, justified dismissal notice.

Can I be banned from working in the UAE if I resign?

If you quit during probation without the employer having breached its obligations, a one-year work-permit ban applies under Ministerial Resolution No. 47 of 2022. See the probation guide.
