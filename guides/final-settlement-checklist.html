Final settlement checklist for leaving a job in the UAE

Your final settlement is the sum of everything your employer owes you when the contract ends. Mistakes are common and are easiest to fix before you sign anything. Use this checklist, then confirm each number with the final settlement calculator.

Before your last day

  1. Confirm the notice. Notice must be in writing and last 30 to 90 days (Article 43). Keep a copy with the date it was received. You receive full wages during the notice period.
  2. Agree your last working day in writing. Your service dates drive gratuity.
  3. Check your contract for the basic salary figure, notice length and any bonus or commission terms.
  4. Count your unused annual leave. Use the annual leave calculator.
  5. Note unpaid leave days; they reduce gratuity service (details).

The deadline

The UAE Government Portal states that the employer must pay outstanding wages, entitlements and gratuity within 14 days of the termination of the contract. Put the date in your calendar: day one is the day after your last working day. If the deadline passes, you can raise a complaint with MOHRE through its official channels.

Worked example: a full settlement

Ayesha has AED 8,000 basic, six years of service, 10 unused leave days and AED 8,000 of salary for her final month. Her gratuity is AED 35,528 (see the calculator example), leave pay is about AED 2,667 and wages are AED 8,000. The settlement should therefore be about AED 46,195 before deductions. If her statement shows less, she asks which line is lower and why.

Reading the statement

  • Is the gratuity based on basic salary and not on a lower figure?
  • Does the service period match your joining and last working dates, less genuine unpaid days?
  • Is the leave balance consistent with your own count?
  • Is every deduction explained, with proof of what you owed?
  • Are bonuses or commissions you earned included?

Before you sign

A signature on a clearance form can be read as accepting the amounts. If a number looks wrong, write down what you believe is owed and ask for the calculation. You can sign to confirm receipt of a payment while noting that you dispute a specific item, but ask for advice if the amount is significant.

Special cases

  • DIFC and some free zones use different schemes (for DIFC, the DEWS savings scheme).
  • Employers on the MOHRE savings scheme pay contributions plus returns within 14 days after employment ends; gratuity accrued before enrolment must still be settled.
  • Part-time or flexible work models: gratuity is the full-time figure multiplied by contracted hours over full-time hours.
  • Dismissal for cause: see resignation vs termination.

Common disputes and how to approach them

  • "Gratuity is calculated on total salary" (or the reverse). The official rule uses basic salary. If your contract splits pay into basic and allowances, the basic figure in the contract is the starting point. Ask for the contract page or salary certificate.
  • Leave balance differences. Compare your own count with the employer's leave record; the annual leave calculator gives an independent figure. The pro rata part-year leave is paid too.
  • Unexplained deductions. Only amounts you actually owe the employer may be deducted from gratuity. Ask for the basis of each deduction, with documents.
  • Late payment. The official deadline is 14 days. Keep proof of your last day and of the date you received payment.
  • Notice period disagreements. Check whether notice was given in writing, how long it was and whether it was served. See notice period pay.

A simple timeline

  1. Weeks before: give written notice, agree the last working day, count leave and unpaid days.
  2. Last week: return company property, ask for a draft settlement statement and a salary certificate if you need one.
  3. Last day: get written confirmation of the last working day.
  4. Within 14 days: payment should arrive. Check each line against your own calculation.
  5. If not paid: write to the employer asking for the payment and the calculation, then escalate through MOHRE channels.

Keep these records

Employment contract and amendments, payslips, resignation or termination letter, leave approvals, the settlement statement, and proof of payment. Keep them for as long as you may need to prove a claim.

Frequently asked questions

When must the employer pay my final settlement?

Within 14 days of the termination of the contract, according to the UAE Government Portal.

Is unused leave paid when I leave?

Yes. The employee is paid for unused annual leave on termination, regardless of its duration, calculated on basic salary.

Can my employer deduct money from my gratuity?

Amounts you owe the employer may be deducted, per the UAE Government Portal.

What if I worked less than a year?

No gratuity is due for less than one year of continuous service, but outstanding wages and unused leave are still payable.
