UAE gratuity examples: what you get at different salaries and years

Formulas are easy to read and hard to picture. This page shows what end-of-service gratuity actually comes to for common basic salaries and lengths of service, and walks through three examples line by line. Every figure comes from the same code as our gratuity calculator, which implements Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 as summarised by the UAE Government Portal.

The rule in one paragraph

For a full-time employee with at least one year of continuous service, gratuity is 21 days of basic salary for each of the first 5 years and 30 days for each year after that. Part-years are paid pro rata once you have passed one year, days of unpaid absence are excluded, and the total cannot exceed two years of wages. The daily wage we use is annual basic salary ÷ 365 (the same method our calculator has always used; see the methodology page).

Example 1: AED 5,000 basic, 3 years

  1. Daily wage = 5,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = AED 164.38.
  2. 3 years × 21 days = 63 days.
  3. 63 × 164.38 = AED 10,356.16.

Compare that with the monthly basic of AED 5,000: three years of service earns about 2.07 months of basic pay.

Example 2: AED 8,000 basic, 8 years

  1. Daily wage = 8,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = AED 263.01.
  2. First 5 years: 5 × 21 = 105 days = AED 27,616.44.
  3. Next 3 years: 3 × 30 = 90 days = AED 23,671.23.
  4. Total = AED 51,287.67 (195 days of wages).

Example 3: AED 12,000 basic, 6 years and 6 months

  1. Daily wage = 12,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = AED 394.52.
  2. First 5 years: 105 days = AED 41,424.66.
  3. Remaining 1.5 years × 30 = 45 days = AED 17,753.42.
  4. Total = AED 59,178.08.

What the examples assume

  • Basic salary only. If your payslip shows AED 12,000 as a package, but only AED 7,000 is basic, use 7,000. This is the most common source of surprise at the end of a job.
  • Continuous service. A year counts as 365 days after unpaid days are removed. See unpaid leave and gratuity.
  • The cap. The total is limited to two years of wages (24 times the monthly basic). At AED 6,000 basic that is AED 144,000 and it is only reached after about 25.8 years of service, so most people never hit it. The cap year is the same at any salary because both the cap and the daily wage scale with basic pay.
  • Not covered here: DIFC employees (DEWS savings scheme), employers on the optional MOHRE savings scheme, part-time work models and UAE nationals (pension system). Part-time gratuity is the full-time figure multiplied by the ratio of contracted hours to full-time hours.

How to use these numbers

Treat them as a sanity check. Take your payslip, find the basic line, read off the matching salary row, and compare it with what your employer's final settlement says. If the gap is large, ask for the calculation in writing: employers are expected to pay all outstanding dues, including gratuity, within 14 days of the end of the contract. Then run the final settlement calculator to add leave pay and notice.

Frequently asked questions

How much gratuity do I get after 5 years at AED 5,000 basic?

17,260 dirhams: 5,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = 164.38 per day, times 105 days.

Why is the table different from my employer's figure?

Common reasons: your employer used total salary instead of basic (which is not allowed under the official rule), a different daily divisor, or unpaid leave days. Ask for the working in writing.

Is there a maximum gratuity?

Yes. The total cannot exceed the wage of two years.

Does the gratuity change if I resign?

The official end-of-service page sets the formula by length of service. See resignation vs termination for what we can and cannot confirm.
