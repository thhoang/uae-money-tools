UAE VAT invoice calculator (multiple lines)

Real invoices mix products. Add one row per line, enter the VAT-exclusive unit price, and mark each line as 5% standard, 0% zero-rated or exempt. The tool totals net, VAT and gross, and shows the value in each category, which you also need for a VAT return.

How the calculation works

For each line: net = quantity × unit price. VAT = net × rate for standard-rated lines, and nil for zero-rated and exempt lines. The invoice totals are the sums of the line figures.

Worked example. 10 hours of consulting at AED 250 (standard): net 2,500, VAT 125. 4 exported items at AED 120 (zero-rated): net 480, VAT 0. Invoice net = AED 2,980, VAT = AED 125, total = AED 3,105.

Zero-rated versus exempt

Both carry no VAT on the invoice, but they differ in how input VAT is treated on your costs. The Federal Tax Authority's VAT guides explain which supplies fall into each group and what a valid tax invoice must show; start with the sources below. For a single amount use the simpler VAT calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in the UAE?

The standard VAT rate is 5%, per the Federal Tax Authority.

Do I charge VAT if I am not registered?

A business that is not VAT-registered does not charge VAT. See the registration threshold guide.

Why show zero-rated and exempt values separately?

VAT returns report standard-rated, zero-rated and exempt supplies separately, so keeping the split on each invoice makes the return easier.
