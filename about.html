About UAE Money Tools

UAE Money Tools is a small independent site with one job: give people living and working in the UAE fast, honest answers to everyday money questions - "how much gratuity am I owed?", "how much of this receipt is VAT?", "what is my salary really worth per hour?".

How we work

  • Official sources only. Every formula and figure comes from published UAE government sources: the UAE Government Portal (u.ae), the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation, the Federal Tax Authority and the General Pension and Social Security Authority. Each guide links its sources.
  • Private by design. All calculations run in your browser. We never see, store or transmit the numbers you type.
  • Free, supported by ads. The site is free to use and will be supported by unobtrusive advertising.

What we are not

We are not a law firm, tax adviser or government body, and nothing here is legal, tax or financial advice. For your specific case, check the official sources linked in each guide or talk to a qualified professional.
