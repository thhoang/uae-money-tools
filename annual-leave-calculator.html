UAE annual leave days calculator

Enter your start date and the date you want to check. Per the UAE Government Portal, you get a fully paid 30 days of annual leave after completing one year, and 2 days for each month of service if you have worked more than six months but less than a year.

Basis-

Estimate only. Pro rata accrual for the part-year uses days of service divided by 365 and then multiplied by 30. Part-time workers accrue on actual hours worked. Your contract may give more than the legal minimum.

The rules this tool follows

  • More than 6 months, under 1 year: 2 days per month of service.
  • 1 year or more: 30 days per year; for the final part-year we pro-rate (days of service ÷ 365 × 30).
  • Under 6 months: we show no accrual because the pages we rely on do not state one. Your contract may allow leave earlier.

Example. Start 1 January 2025, as of 1 July 2026: 546 days of service = 1.4959 years × 30 = 44.88 days accrued. If you have taken 10 days, 34.88 remain. Unused days are paid in cash when employment ends; use the leave encashment calculator to value them.

Carry-over

With the employer's consent, unused leave can be carried over to the next year. The UAE Government Portal also says an employer cannot stop an employee using accrued leave beyond two consecutive years unless the employee chooses to carry it over or take cash compensation.

Frequently asked questions

How many annual leave days do I get in the UAE?

30 days per year after one year of service, per the UAE Government Portal. For service between six months and one year it is 2 days per month.

Do I get leave during probation?

The employer may grant annual leave during probation, and if you do not pass probation you are entitled to compensation for the remaining annual leave balance, according to the Decree-Law summary on the UAE Government Portal.

Are weekends and public holidays counted in the 30 days?

The pages we rely on do not say, so this calculator does not adjust for them. Check your contract and MOHRE.
