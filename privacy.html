Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing you type into a calculator leaves your device. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser with JavaScript. We do not operate a server that receives your salary, dates or amounts, and we have no accounts, sign-ups or contact forms that store personal data.

Server logs

Like every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data when your browser requests a page - IP address, browser type and similar - to deliver content and protect the site. We do not use this data to identify you.

Advertising

The site is designed to be supported by advertising. If ads are enabled in future (for example through Google AdSense), the ad provider may use cookies to serve and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. When that happens we will update this page with the provider's details and a link to its opt-out. You can always block cookies in your browser settings.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly, aggregate traffic statistics to understand which tools are useful. We do not build individual profiles.

Links

We link to official UAE government sites (u.ae, mohre.gov.ae, tax.gov.ae and others). Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
