How to use it

Savings rate = (income − spending) ÷ income. Example: income AED 15,000 and costs of AED 9,000 leave AED 6,000, a 40% savings rate and AED 72,000 over a year.

Tips for a realistic budget

  • Use last month's bank statement rather than memory.
  • List yearly costs (insurance, school re-enrolment, visa renewals) divided by 12.
  • Keep "money sent home" on its own line so it is visible.
  • If you are leaving a job, plan around your final settlement arriving later than your last salary.

Frequently asked questions

Is there income tax in the UAE?

This tool does not model taxes. It takes your take-home income as an input; our salary calculator helps you estimate take-home pay.

Does this site store my budget?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent.
