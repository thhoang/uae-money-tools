Frequently asked questions

What is the VAT rate in the UAE?

5% since 1 January 2018. A limited set of goods and services is zero-rated (exports, international transport, certain education and healthcare) or exempt (bare land, local passenger transport, some financial services).

How do I work out the VAT inside a receipt total?

Divide the total by 1.05 to get the net price; the difference is the VAT. For AED 105, the net is AED 100 and the VAT is AED 5.

When must a business register for VAT?

Mandatory above AED 375,000 of taxable supplies and imports per year; voluntary above AED 187,500.

Do tourists pay VAT?

Yes, but they can reclaim it on eligible purchases at the airport when leaving, through the official tourist refund scheme.
