UAE overtime pay calculator

Enter your basic salary and the extra hours you worked. Overtime is paid at your normal hourly rate (based on basic salary) plus 25%, rising to plus 50% for overtime between 10 pm and 4 am. Work on an off-day is paid at the normal rate plus 50% (or you get a substitute rest day instead).

The official sources say pay is based on basic salary but do not fix the daily divisor, so you can choose. Check what your contract or MOHRE says for your case.

How the calculation works

Hourly rate = basic monthly salary ÷ days per month ÷ normal hours per day. Overtime pay = hourly rate × 1.25 for ordinary overtime, × 1.5 for 10 pm to 4 am overtime or off-day work.

Worked example. Basic AED 6,000, 30 days, 8 hours: hourly rate = 6,000 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = AED 25.00. Four daytime overtime hours = 4 × 25 × 1.25 = AED 125. Two night hours = 2 × 25 × 1.5 = AED 75. One off-day hour = 1 × 25 × 1.5 = AED 37.50. Total: AED 237.50.

What the UAE Government Portal says

  • Normal working hours are 8 per day or 48 per week (Article 17).
  • Overtime cannot exceed 2 extra hours per day.
  • Overtime is paid at the normal hourly rate based on basic salary plus at least 25%, or plus 50% between 10 pm and 4 am. These rates do not apply to shift workers.
  • Work on an off-day earns either a substitute rest day or pay at the normal rate plus 50%.
  • In Ramadan, normal hours are reduced by 2 hours a day; see working hours in Ramadan.

Frequently asked questions

How much is overtime pay in the UAE?

The normal hourly rate based on basic salary plus 25%, or plus 50% for overtime between 10 pm and 4 am, according to the UAE Government Portal.

Is overtime based on basic or total salary?

The UAE Government Portal says the hourly rate is based on basic salary.

What is the maximum overtime per day?

Overtime cannot exceed 2 extra hours per day.

Do shift workers get these overtime rates?

No. The UAE Government Portal says the overtime rates do not apply to shift workers.
