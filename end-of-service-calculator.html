UAE final settlement (end of service) calculator

Your last payment is usually more than gratuity. This tool adds the four common parts: gratuity, unused annual leave, a notice shortfall if either side did not serve notice, and outstanding wages, then subtracts anything you owe. Contract type does not change the formula: since 2022 the same Article 51 gratuity rules apply, as explained in our limited vs unlimited contracts guide.

What goes into the total

  1. Gratuity. 21 days of basic pay for each of the first 5 years and 30 days for each year after, pro rata, capped at two years' wages. Unpaid leave days are excluded. See the gratuity calculator and methodology.
  2. Unused leave. Paid on basic salary when employment ends.
  3. Notice shortfall. If the employer ended the contract without notice you may be owed an allowance; if you left without serving notice you may owe one.
  4. Wages and other dues. Salary for the last days worked and anything else already earned.

The employer must pay outstanding wages and entitlements, including gratuity, within 14 days of the end of the contract (UAE Government Portal).

Worked example. Basic AED 8,000; 1 January 2019 to 31 December 2024 (2,191 days = 6.0027 years); 10 unused leave days at 30 days a month; no notice issue; AED 8,000 unpaid salary. Daily wage = 8,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = AED 263.01. Gratuity: 105 days (5 years × 21) = 27,616.44, plus 1.0027 years × 30 = 30.08 days = 7,912.03, so AED 35,528.47. Leave: 10 × 266.67 = AED 2,666.67. Unpaid salary: AED 8,000. Total AED 46,195.13 before any deductions.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in a UAE final settlement?

Typically outstanding wages, unused annual leave, gratuity if you have at least one year of service, and any notice allowance. Use our checklist to verify each line.

How long does the employer have to pay?

Within 14 days of the end of the contract, per the UAE Government Portal.

Does the contract type change my gratuity?

No. The same Article 51 formula is applied; unlimited-term contracts had to be converted to fixed-term by the end of 2023.

Can my employer deduct money?

Amounts you owe the employer may be deducted from gratuity, according to the UAE Government Portal.
