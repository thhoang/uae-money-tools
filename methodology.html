Methodology: formulas and assumptions

This page lists every formula used on the site so you can check it. The same code (assets/calc.js) powers the calculators and the pre-computed tables, and it is tested against hand-calculated examples. Where an official source does not fix a convention, the page says so and the calculator lets you choose.

Gratuity (Article 51, Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021)

  • Service years = (days from first to last day − unpaid-leave days) ÷ 365.
  • Less than 1 year: gratuity = 0.
  • Daily wage = basic monthly salary × 12 ÷ 365.
  • Gratuity = daily wage × (21 × min(years, 5) + 30 × max(years − 5, 0)).
  • Cap: gratuity cannot exceed 24 × basic monthly salary (two years' wages). The cap is reached at 5 + (730 − 105) ÷ 30 = about 25.83 years of service, whatever the salary.

Assumption: the official sources say gratuity uses basic salary and a daily wage; the 365-day divisor is our convention. Part-years are pro rata. Part-time work models (full-time gratuity × contracted hours ÷ full-time hours) are not modelled in the calculator.

Unused annual leave

Pay = (basic monthly salary ÷ D) × unused days, where D is 30 or 30.42 (365 ÷ 12). The sources specify basic salary but not D, so both choices are offered.

Annual leave accrual

  • Under 6 months: no accrual shown (not stated in the pages we use).
  • 6 to 12 months: 2 days × completed months of service.
  • 1 year or more: 30 days × service days ÷ 365.

Notice allowance

Allowance = (monthly wage ÷ D) × max(required notice days − days served, 0). Article 43 sets notice at 30 to 90 days with full wages during the period and an allowance for the unserved part; the per-day conversion is our simplification.

Overtime

Hourly rate = basic ÷ D ÷ normal hours per day. Daytime overtime = hours × hourly rate × 1.25. Overtime from 10 pm to 4 am and work on an off-day (if paid instead of a substitute rest day) = hours × hourly rate × 1.5. These multipliers come from the UAE Government Portal's working-hours page.

VAT

  • Add VAT: VAT = net × rate; gross = net + VAT.
  • Remove VAT: net = gross ÷ (1 + rate); VAT = gross − net.
  • Invoice lines: net = quantity × unit price, rounded to the nearest fils; VAT = net × rate on standard lines, nil on zero-rated and exempt lines.
  • Standard rate: 5% (Federal Tax Authority).

Rounding

Intermediate values are not rounded; results are displayed to two decimals (except per-line invoice VAT, which is rounded per line). Differences of a few fils against an employer's figure usually come from the daily-wage divisor or rounding.

Testing

The repository contains TESTS.md with hand-computed examples and a script that checks the code against them.
