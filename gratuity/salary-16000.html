Gratuity for AED 16,000 basic salary (UAE)

Your daily wage for gratuity purposes at AED 16,000 basic is AED 526.03. That one number drives every figure on this page: 21 of those days per year for the first five years, 30 after. These figures apply the Article 51 formula used by the UAE Government Portal: 21 days of basic pay per year for the first five years, 30 days per year after that, capped at two years' wages, and nothing for less than one year of continuous service.

What these numbers tell you at AED 16,000

  • The first five years add AED 11,046.58 each. After year five, each extra year adds AED 15,780.82.
  • Ten years of service pays AED 134,137 (8.4 months of basic). Twenty years pays AED 291,945 (18.2 months).
  • The cap (two years of wages) is AED 384,000; it is reached at about 25.8 years of service.
  • Each extra AED 1,000 of basic adds roughly AED 3,452 to a five-year payout. At AED 17,000 basic, five years pays AED 58,685. At AED 15,000 basic it pays AED 51,781.

Worked example: AED 16,000 basic, 12.75 years

  1. Daily wage = 16,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = AED 526.03.
  2. First 5 years at 21 days = 105 days = AED 55,232.88.
  3. Remaining 7.75 years at 30 days = 232.5 days = AED 122,301.37.
  4. Total gratuity = AED 177,534.25.

Check before you rely on this

Use basic salary only, not housing or transport allowances. Remove any unpaid leave days from your service. DIFC and some free zones, part-time work models and UAE nationals follow different rules; see the methodology. Your employer must pay final dues, including gratuity, within 14 days of the end of the contract. For leave pay and notice, use the final settlement calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much is gratuity on AED 16,000 basic salary?

After 1 year: AED 11,046.58. After 5 years: AED 55,232.88. After 10 years: AED 134,136.99. See the full table above.

What is the daily wage at AED 16,000 basic?

AED 526.03: 16,000 × 12 ÷ 365.

Is there a maximum for AED 16,000 basic?

The cap is two years' wages: AED 384,000. It is reached after about 25.8 years of service.
