Gratuity for AED 6,000 basic salary (UAE)

Planning to leave a job paying AED 6,000 basic? After one year you are owed about AED 4,142; after five, AED 20,712. The table below runs to 30 years. These figures apply the Article 51 formula used by the UAE Government Portal: 21 days of basic pay per year for the first five years, 30 days per year after that, capped at two years' wages, and nothing for less than one year of continuous service.

What these numbers tell you at AED 6,000

  • The first five years add AED 4,142.47 each. After year five, each extra year adds AED 5,917.81.
  • Ten years of service pays AED 50,301 (8.4 months of basic). Twenty years pays AED 109,479 (18.2 months).
  • The cap (two years of wages) is AED 144,000; it is reached at about 25.8 years of service.
  • Each extra AED 1,000 of basic adds roughly AED 3,452 to a five-year payout. At AED 7,000 basic, five years pays AED 24,164. At AED 5,000 basic it pays AED 17,260.

Worked example: AED 6,000 basic, 8 years

  1. Daily wage = 6,000 × 12 ÷ 365 = AED 197.26.
  2. First 5 years at 21 days = 105 days = AED 20,712.33.
  3. Remaining 3 years at 30 days = 90 days = AED 17,753.42.
  4. Total gratuity = AED 38,465.75.

Check before you rely on this

Use basic salary only, not housing or transport allowances. Remove any unpaid leave days from your service. DIFC and some free zones, part-time work models and UAE nationals follow different rules; see the methodology. Your employer must pay final dues, including gratuity, within 14 days of the end of the contract. For leave pay and notice, use the final settlement calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much is gratuity on AED 6,000 basic salary?

After 1 year: AED 4,142.47. After 5 years: AED 20,712.33. After 10 years: AED 50,301.37. See the full table above.

What is the daily wage at AED 6,000 basic?

AED 197.26: 6,000 × 12 ÷ 365.

Is there a maximum for AED 6,000 basic?

The cap is two years' wages: AED 144,000. It is reached after about 25.8 years of service.
