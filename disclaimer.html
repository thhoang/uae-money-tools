Estimates only

Calculators give estimates based on rules as we understand them from the official sources linked on each page. Your actual entitlement depends on your contract, your employer's policies, your free zone, and the facts of your case. Results can differ from what your employer or a court decides.

Laws change

Each page shows the date its sources were last checked. Laws, fees and ministry guidance change, and we may not have updated a page yet. Confirm anything important with the linked official source before you rely on it.

Where we are unsure

When official pages disagree or do not give a figure, we say so and leave the figure out instead of guessing. If you spot an error, please tell us.

Who it does not cover

Gratuity pages are written for full-time private-sector expatriate employees under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021. DIFC, some other free zones, government employees, domestic workers and UAE nationals may be covered by different rules.
