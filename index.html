Why these tools

Each rule comes from a published UAE government source (the UAE Government Portal, MOHRE, the Federal Tax Authority), is cited on the page with a "last checked" date, and is applied in a way you can follow step by step. Where the official pages do not give a figure, we say so rather than guess.
