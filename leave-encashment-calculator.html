UAE leave encashment calculator

When your job ends, your employer must pay you for annual leave you did not take. This calculator turns your unused days into a cash figure using your basic salary, which is the basis the UAE Government Portal gives for this payment.

How the calculation works

Daily rate = basic monthly salary ÷ days per month. Leave pay = daily rate × unused days. For a basic salary of AED 6,000 and 12 unused days at 30 days per month: 6,000 ÷ 30 = AED 200 per day, and 200 × 12 = AED 2,400.

What the official sources say

  • On termination, the employee is paid for unused annual leave regardless of its duration.
  • Leave accrued for the part of the year worked is also paid.
  • The payment is calculated on the basis of basic salary.

Which divisor your employer uses (30 days, or annual salary ÷ 365) is not set out in the pages we rely on, so both are offered. The difference is small but real: on AED 6,000 and 12 days it is about AED 31.

Frequently asked questions

Do I get paid for unused annual leave when I resign?

The UAE Government Portal says the employee is paid for unused annual leave on termination of employment, whatever the duration of the unused leave. That covers the end of the contract in general; see our final settlement checklist.

Is leave pay based on basic or total salary?

The UAE Government Portal says it is calculated on the basis of basic salary.

Can I carry unused leave into next year?

With the employer's consent, unused leave can be carried over to the following year, according to the UAE Government Portal.
