Contact

Found an error in a calculation or an outdated rule? Suggesting a new tool? The fastest way to reach us is through the project's public issue tracker on GitHub:

github.com/thhoang/uae-money-tools/issues →

When reporting a calculation problem, please include the numbers you entered and the result you expected - it makes fixes much faster.

A note on personal cases

We cannot advise on individual employment or tax situations. For those, contact MOHRE (80060 in the UAE) for labour questions or the Federal Tax Authority for VAT questions.
